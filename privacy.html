Privacy Policy

Last updated September 2, 2026

This Privacy Policy is issued by Sricharan Pottabathula, operating as FirstCall Automation, in connection with the automated lead response system described on this site.

This privacy policy explains how we collect and use information submitted through our contact forms.

Information we collect

When you submit a form on this site, we collect your name, phone number, email address, and a description of your request.

How we use your information

We use this information solely to respond to your request and follow up by phone, text, or email regarding the issue you described.

Sharing your information

We do not sell, rent, or share your phone number or any other personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.

Text messaging

If you provide a phone number, you may receive automated text messages confirming your request. Message frequency varies depending on your interaction with us, typically one to three messages per request. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP at any time to opt out, or reply HELP for assistance.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to contact.firstcallautomation@gmail.com.
